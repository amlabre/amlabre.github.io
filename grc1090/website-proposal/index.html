Introduction

Press Start

Press Start is a preowned game console and video game store. Press Start sells both refurbished and original consoles and accessories at affordable prices. Customers can also sell their own consoles and games in-store for store credit or cash.

Target audience

Press Start's target audience is thrifty video game enthusiasts. The target demographic is wide as both new and retro consoles and video games are sold at Press Start. Both older audiences and younger audiences would be attracted to the site.

People seeking to purchase, trade-in or sell their consoles, accessories and/or games would visit the site. These customers would want to know the price of the items of interest as well as their condition. Trade-in or selling information should be easy to find and cohesive. Contact information for the store should be easy to find for customers who need estimates or have general questions about the store and inventory.
